Welcome to the Stimulated Raman Adiabatic Passage (STIRAP) Learning Tool!

Developers: Brayton Bosuku, Miguel Alarcon, Nikolay Golubev

This tool demonstrates how STIRAP, which is a way to transfer a population of electrons from one level to the next, interacts with certain parameters, as well as how changes to these items affect the graph as a whole.
